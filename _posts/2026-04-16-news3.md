Dr. Ning Wang has been named a Faculty Fellow in the Molecular Sciences Software
Institute's (MolSSI) [Accelerating Curricular Transformation in the Computational
Molecular Sciences (ACT-CMS)](https://molssi.org/fellowship/act-cms-faculty-fellows/)
program.

ACT-CMS is a national faculty development initiative, funded by the National
Science Foundation, that supports educators integrating programming, data
competency, and computational skills into molecular science curricula. As a
Faculty Fellow, Dr. Wang will work with MolSSI mentors to build open, reusable course modules that bring Python and computation into chemical engineering classrooms.
